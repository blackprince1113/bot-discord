Distrion, Heleen
Distrion, born in Bilbao, Spain, is formed by Ander Vicente, a young next generation DJ & Producer. He started producing when he was only 13 and his career started to kick off when he started his project “Distrion” a few years ago. Now, after 4 years, he has releases on labels like NCS, DHARMA, REVEALED RECORDINGS… and his tracks are already supported by big names like The Chainsmokers, KSHMR, Martin Garrix, Mike Williams and a lot more. Passion and motivation is what started Distrion’s dream, determination and discipline is what keeps it going.
